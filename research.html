Research

My research lies at the intersection of differential geometry and partial differential equations, with a focus on geometric flows and the formation of singularities.

Mean curvature flow

I study analytic and geometric aspects of mean curvature flow, including well-posedness, curvature estimates, ancient solutions, and singularity formation under integral or index bounds.

Analysis on evolving manifolds

My work also concerns analytic estimates on time-dependent geometric backgrounds, including Calderón–Zygmund inequalities and covariant Riesz transforms.

Current themes

  • Singularity models and compactness for mean curvature flow
  • Curvature integral bounds and regularity
  • Geometric differential operators on Riemannian manifolds

  1. Mean curvature flow in general ambient manifolds, 2026.
    PDF
    Abstract

    We establish short-time existence of mean curvature flow starting from a complete immersed submanifold with bounded second fundamental form in a complete ambient manifold. The ambient curvature and its first two covariant derivatives are assumed to be bounded, and no positive lower bound for the injectivity radius is required. The existence time and the curvature bound depend only on these bounds and the dimensions.

  2. Singularities of mean curvature flow with bounded total curvature
    with Zhen Wang, 2026.
    arXiv
    Abstract

    We prove that every tangent flow at the first singular time of a smooth, closed, embedded mean curvature flow has smooth support, provided that the total curvature satisfies \(\sup_{t\in[0,T)}\int_{M_t}|A|^n\,d\mu_t<\infty\). This extends Ilmanen's smoothness result for tangent flows to higher dimensions.

    Abstract

  5. The covariant Riesz transforms on Riemannian manifolds
    with Bing Wang, 2026.
    arXiv
    Abstract

    We establish the \(L^p\)-boundedness of the local covariant Riesz transform for differential forms on manifold \(M\) with bounded \(\|Rm\|\). Let \(\Delta_j\) be the Hodge Laplace operator on \(j\)-forms. For any \(p \in (1, \infty)\) and \(\kappa>\kappa_0\), we show that the operator \(\nabla (\Delta_j + \kappa)^{-1/2}\) is bounded on \(L^p(M)\). Consequently, we obtain Calderón-Zygmund estimates for manifolds with bounded Riemannian curvature.

  6. Singularity of mean curvature flow with bounded mean curvature and Morse index, 2025.
    arXiv
    Abstract

    We study the multiplicity of the singularities of mean curvature flow with bounded mean curvature and Morse index. For \(3\leq n\leq 6\), we show that either the mean curvature or the Morse index blows up at the first singular time for a closed smooth embedded mean curvature flow in \(\mathbb{R}^{n+1}\).

  2. Mean curvature flow with pinched curvature integral.
    Differential Geometry and its Applications 99 (2025).
    Abstract

    If \(\Sigma\) is an \(n\)-dimensional noncompact self-shrinker and the second fundamental form of \(\Sigma\) is \(L^p\) integrable for \(p\ge n\), we show that \(\Sigma\) is asymptotic to a regular cone. We also prove long-time existence of the mean curvature flow starting from complete manifolds with bounded curvature and small total curvature.

  3. Constructions of minimal Hermitian matrices related to a C*-subalgebra of Mn(ℂ).
    with Y. Zhang and L. Jiang. Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society 151(1), 73–84 (2023).
    Abstract

    This paper provides a constructive method using unitary diagonalizable elements to obtain all hermitian matrices \(A\) in \(M_n(\mathbb{C})\) such that \(\|A\|=\min_{B\in\mathcal{B}}\|A+B\|\), where \(\mathcal{B}\) is a C*-subalgebra of \(M_n(\mathbb{C})\), \(\|\cdot\|\) denotes the operator norm. Such an \(A\) is called \(\mathcal{B}\)-minimal. Moreover, for a C*-subalgebra \(\mathcal{B}\) determined by a conditional expectation from \(M_n(\mathbb{C})\) onto it, this paper constructs \(\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}\mathcal{B}\)-minimal hermitian matrices in \(M_{kn}(\mathbb{C})\) through \(\mathcal{B}\)-minimal hermitian matrices in \(M_n(\mathbb{C})\), and gets a dominated condition that the matrix \(\widehat{A}=\operatorname{diag}(A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_k)\) is \(\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}\mathcal{B}\)-minimal if and only if \(\|\widehat{A}\|\leq\|A_s\|\) for some \(s\in\{1,2,\ldots,k\}\) and \(A_s\) is \(\mathcal{B}\)-minimal, where \(A_i\) \((1\leq i\leq k)\) are hermitian matrices in \(M_n(\mathbb{C})\).
